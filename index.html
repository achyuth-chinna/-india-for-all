A policy for public discussion

Two universal guarantees to examine

Free at the point of use does not mean free to provide. Any universal guarantee requires sustainable public financing, staffing, quality standards and transparent accountability.

🎓

Education from LKG to advanced study

Examine a publicly funded pathway from early childhood through school, vocational training, college, professional education and research, subject to transparent academic standards.

  • Reduce fee-related barriers
  • Invest in teachers and learning quality
  • Improve rural and underserved access
🩺

Healthcare for every medical need

Examine comprehensive, medically necessary care with no patient charges, including consultations, medicines, diagnostics, surgeries, chronic illness and rehabilitation.

  • Prevent medical financial hardship
  • Strengthen public hospitals
  • Contract and regulate private providers

What could improve?

  • Greater access to education and qualifications
  • Earlier diagnosis and continuity of treatment
  • Less household exposure to medical bills
  • Potential long-term gains in opportunity and productivity

What must be solved?

  • Cost, taxation and Union–state funding
  • Doctors, teachers, facilities and waiting times
  • Standards for private hospital participation
  • Protecting essential food, disability and pension support

Methodology matters

Numbers should inform, not mislead.

A daily research digest can report new publications. It cannot turn annual crime statistics or mortality surveys into verified daily incident counts.

Our evidence standard: We distinguish registered crime from its causes; audit observations from confirmed waste; hospital price complaints from proven misconduct; and observed deaths from estimates of avoidable mortality. Education and healthcare can address important problems, but they are not the only factors behind every social outcome.
