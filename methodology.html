India for All is an independent public-interest initiative advocating discussion of universally accessible, publicly funded education and medically necessary healthcare. It is not affiliated with the Government of India.

1. Primary sources

Prioritise Indian government reports, official statistical series, audited budgets, and peer-reviewed research. Record report title, publisher, coverage, period and URL.

2. No invented precision

Do not label annual data as today's count. Distinguish enrolment, attendance, quality, expenditure, and outcomes; avoid unsupported causal conclusions.

3. Review before publishing

Daily research summaries are not automatically verified facts. New datasets should pass a source and definition check before appearing on public dashboards.

4. Fair comparisons

Compare welfare programmes on evidence, costs and distributional impacts. Audit observations are not automatically proven fraud or recoverable savings.
