Privacy and community rules

Public participation

Voting is optional. Your name and city stay private unless you explicitly agree to show them with your vote. We do not ask for an electoral ID, phone number or email. This is a browser-based opinion poll, not verified unique people or an election.

Comments and replies are public only after you agree to publish them. Use an alias if you prefer. Do not post private contact details, medical records, threats, harassment or other sensitive information. Comments remain in the language written by their authors.

Your browser and your choices

A signed participation token is stored in your browser for up to 180 days to manage your vote, reactions and comments. Clearing browser storage or using another device can create another identity; counts cannot prove one person, one vote. Your language and analytics preferences are also stored locally.

Use Withdraw my vote to remove your vote and public profile. You can remove your own comment text and details from the same browser; replies remain. Moderators can hide or restore other comments. Reporting a comment sends it for review and does not guarantee immediate removal.

Optional visitor statistics

Statistics are off until you agree. If enabled, we record a rotating daily browser identifier, page, country or broad region, device category, visit times and page views. Raw IP addresses, exact location and names are not stored in these statistics. Visitor rows and daily totals are retained for up to 30 days.

The private owner dashboard refreshes every 30 seconds. Active means a consenting browser seen within the last five minutes. Location is approximate and counts exclude people who decline statistics, blockers and some bots. Cloudflare still processes network information to deliver and protect the website.

Public comments and votes are stored until withdrawn, removed or moderated. A hidden comment may be retained for moderation; removing your own comment erases its text and public details. Never post anything you would not want others to copy.
